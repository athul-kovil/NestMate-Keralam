# 🌴 NestMate Kerala — Roommate & Shared Flat Matching Platform

A dedicated web platform designed for finding compatible roommates and shared flats in **Kerala, India** (Kochi / Kakkanad Infopark, Trivandrum / Technopark, Kozhikode / Cyberpark, Thrissur, etc.).

---

## 🚀 What Was Updated per Your Request

1. **Focused 100% on Kerala, India**:
   - Currency set to **₹ (INR)** across all listings, rent split, deposits, and budget sliders.
   - Pre-configured with top Kerala IT and residential hubs:
     - **Kochi**: Kakkanad (Infopark Phase 1 & 2), Edappally, Palarivattom, Kaloor, Vyttila, Aluva, Fort Kochi.
     - **Trivandrum**: Kazhakkoottam (Technopark Phase 1, 2, 3), Karyavattom, Kowdiar, Pattom, Vellayambalam.
     - **Kozhikode (Calicut)**: Cyberpark, Hilite City / Palazhi, Palayam, Mavoor Road.
     - **Thrissur, Kottayam, Palakkad, Kannur, Kollam**.
   - Working routines customized for Kerala IT shifts, WFH, and colleges (CUSAT, CET, NITC, MEC).

2. **100% Fresh Start (No Dummy Sample Data)**:
   - All sample mock rooms and dummy conversations have been **completely removed**.
   - The platform starts with a **clean database** so you can post your real room with actual photos, actual rent split, and actual phone number.
   - Clean empty state with high-converting call-to-actions: *"Start Fresh: No rooms posted yet in Kerala — Post your room now!"*.

3. **Direct Google Maps Integration**:
   - **Interactive Live Google Map View**: Full Google Maps embedded view centered on Kerala with one-click toggles for Infopark, Technopark, and Cyberpark.
   - **Room Details Map**: Each room listing displays a live Google Map iframe showing the exact location and building.
   - **"🗺️ Open in Google Maps" Button**: Opens the exact location directly in the Google Maps app or website.
   - **"🚗 Get Directions" Button**: Direct route navigation on Google Maps from your current location to the room.
   - **Form Map Preview**: When posting a room, typing your Kerala area and building immediately updates the live Google Map preview in the form.

4. **Real Photo Uploading**:
   - Directly upload multiple photos from your mobile phone or computer (supports JPG, PNG, WEBP).
   - Instant visual thumbnail previews with delete buttons before publishing.
   - Photos persist in browser `localStorage`.

5. **Promotion & Viral WhatsApp Sharing**:
   - **"📲 Share on WhatsApp" Button**: Formats a message with the room title, location, rent split in ₹, habits, and direct link so you can share directly to WhatsApp groups, college alumni groups, and flatmate networks.
   - **"📋 Copy Share Link"**: Copies formatted room details and link to clipboard with one click.
   - **Top Promotion Banner**: Encourages sharing to get more roommates and visitors fast.
